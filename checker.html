Leakage checker

This is case 1 turned into something you can run. Give it a feature table and tell it which column is the label. It looks for pairs of columns that are harmless on their own and together rebuild the thing you are trying to predict.

Everything runs in your browser. Your file is never uploaded and there is no server to upload it to.

The threshold is the cut in your label definition. If your label is "the ratio fell below 0.6", the threshold is 0.6.

What the example is

The example loads a 961 row feature table where I planted the exact failure from case 1: the numerator and the denominator of the label ratio, taken from the outcome month and renamed to something innocent. No single column in it correlates with the label above 0.16, so a column-by-column review passes it. The pair reproduces the label on every row.
